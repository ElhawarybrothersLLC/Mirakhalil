About

The Woman Behind the Roles

Mira Khalil is an Egyptian actress, fashion model, lawyer, educational advisor, and content creator born in 1989. She began her career in 2008 as a runway and fashion model with Face to Face Agency in Cairo, and in 2010 — at just 21 years old — she was crowned First Runner-Up at Miss Egypt 2010.

While pursuing the spotlight, Mira simultaneously earned her Bachelor of Laws (LLB) from the Faculty of Law at Alexandria University (2006–2010), practiced as a lawyer at Gamil Essa Office for International Law, and became a member of the Union Internationale des Avocats (UIA).

Her acting career spans 15 credited film and television roles between 2009 and 2018, including standout performances in "Ghesh El Zawjiya" (2012), "Hidden Worlds" (2018) alongside Adel Imam, and "Ali Baba" (2018). She also created and hosted "Bent Khalil" (بنت خليل), a satirical YouTube commentary show on Ramadan TV dramas.

Since 2019, Mira has channeled her multilingual skills — Arabic, English, Italian, German, French, and Spanish — into the international education sector, serving as an Educational Advisor and Enrollments Manager at Istituto Marangoni, one of the world's premier fashion and design schools.

"I never chose between my passions — I chose to master them all."

My Journey — From Alexandria to the world stage

2006

Law Studies Begin

Enrolled at Faculty of Law, Alexandria University — beginning a 4-year journey to earn her Bachelor of Laws (LLB) degree.

2008

Modeling Career

Signed with Face to Face Agency in Cairo, beginning her career as a runway and fashion model while still studying law.

2009

First Acting Role

Made her television debut in the Egyptian sitcom "Beet El Eila" (بيت العيلة) — launching a decade-long acting career.

2010

Miss Egypt & Law Degree

Crowned 1st Runner-Up at Miss Egypt 2010, and graduated with LLB from Alexandria University — all at age 21. Began practicing law at Gamil Essa Office for International Law.

2012

Breakout Film Roles

Starred as Elena, the Italian manager, in "Ghesh El Zawjiya" (غش الزوجية) and "Inside the Game" (جوه اللعبة) — her most prominent film roles.

2015

Berlin Chapter

Moved to Berlin, Germany for a content moderation role at Arvato Bertelsmann — expanding her international experience and language skills.

2017

Triple Series Year

Appeared in three series: "Khalsana Be Shyaka", "3oshem Eblees", and "Hekayat Banat 3" — while pivoting to education as Student Coordinator at Regents Education.

2018

Peak Acting Year

Appeared in "Hidden Worlds" (عوالم خفية) with Adel Imam, "Azmi & Ashgan", "Ali Baba", and "We Have Further Comments" — her most prolific year on screen.

2019

YouTube Creator

Launched "Bent Khalil" (بنت خليل), a satirical YouTube show offering sharp commentary on Ramadan TV dramas — a viral hit with audiences.

2019

Istituto Marangoni

Joined Istituto Marangoni as Educational Advisor & Student Coordinator — bringing her multilingual skills to international education management.

2020

Published Author

Co-authored "Fraudsters are exploiting blockchains and digital currencies" in ACFE Fraud Magazine — bridging her legal knowledge with emerging technology.

2026

Hawary AI Partner

Joined Hawary AI as operations partner — combining her legal expertise and tech skills to build AI-powered legal solutions for the MENA region.

Miss Egypt 2010 — First Runner-Up

On July 7, 2010, at Studio 1 in Media Production City, Cairo, Mira Khalil was crowned First Runner-Up at the Miss Egypt 2010 pageant — organized by Face 2 Face Agency. She was just 21 years old, competing alongside future Miss Egypt Universe Donia Hamed and Miss Egypt World Sara El-Khouly.
